If the darkness that follows me ever catches me unprepared, if I go down again, or if the noise inside my head finally drowns out who I used to be, I need this here.

I never wanted to forget you two.

Consider this both a monument and a memento of what we built, and a way to keep my own ghost honest.

Details might fade, but what we had was real.
